SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Computer Engineering (CPE) is the architectural bridge between Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, designing the heartbeat of modern technology.

The Core Integration

Computer Engineering integrates Hardware Design and Software Development. Unlike pure CS which focuses on algorithms, or pure EE which focuses on power, CPE lives in the intersection—ensuring that physical circuits and digital code work in perfect harmony.
